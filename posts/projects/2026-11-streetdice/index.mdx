**React dice game** | November 2026 - Present

## Overview

**Street Dice** is a two-player browser game built around the feeling of a late-night sidewalk game: a dark table, a couple of dice, loose bills, and just enough noise around the roll to make the next throw feel important.

The game keeps the familiar structure of simplified street craps. Players choose whether the higher or lower opening roll wins the shooter, roll the come-out, establish a point when needed, and keep rolling until the shooter hits the point or throws seven. Bets are represented as paper bills rather than a sterile amount field, with both players' money landing in a visible pot.

## Making the Table Feel Human

The visual language is intentionally rough around the edges:

- dark concrete and faded brick instead of casino green;
- hand-lettered memorial tape for Takeoff;
- paper-money cards for $1, $5, $10, $20, $50, and $100;
- a pot that shows who has actually put money down;
- side-bet markings for future house rules without pretending they are live yet.

The goal was not to make a casino simulator. It was to make the little game between rolls feel like it has a place, a history, and two people leaning over it.

## Rules and State

The game follows the standard simplified craps outcomes after the shooter is chosen:

- **7 or 11** on the come-out wins immediately;
- **2, 3, or 12** loses immediately;
- **4, 5, 6, 8, 9, or 10** establishes the point;
- the point wins, a **7** loses, and every other total keeps the point alive.

The state model keeps shooter identity separate from the player currently placing a bet. That distinction matters: the player rolling the dice should receive the win, while a losing shooter should hand the pot to the other player.
